# Coaching demo rehearsal

Use the candidate app at <http://127.0.0.1:5173>. Refresh after a candidate
restart and choose **New Session** on Bob's card. Saved sessions keep their
earlier prompts and feedback. Do not resume one to demonstrate the new coach.

Suggested opening narration:

> I'm rehearsing a difficult conversation about missed deadlines. Bob has his
> own perspective. The coach helps me decide what to say next based on his reply.

Start with:

> Bob, could you walk me through what's making this deadline difficult?

Let Bob finish. Request a hint and show the **Try saying** line. Read it against
what Bob actually said, then use it if it fits. Continue for a few exchanges,
letting his answer determine the next hint. Request a second approach once to
show an alternative; requesting it does not send either line to Bob.

Narrate what the conversation actually demonstrates: an issue becoming clearer,
a concern being addressed, a decision needed or a conditional next step. If Bob
asks for a pause, accept it. There is no target score or required disclosure.

Show the feedback evidence if relevant. Describe stance as a simulated response
and acknowledgment as an AI judgment of this rehearsal, not a measurement of
real trust, empathy or competence. A provider failure should show its actual
fallback or unavailable state; the conversation remains saved.

End by showing the saved conversation in the recorder. Rewind/branch only if
needed to illustrate trying another approach; preserve the original exchange.

For rehearsal acceptance, try one new conversation using only in-app hints.
Record whether each suggestion is easy to say, responds to Bob, avoids repeating
answered questions and helps you choose a next move. A higher score or longer
Bob reply alone is not acceptance.
